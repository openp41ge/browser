## What's new in 0.1.6

- Your windows and tabs come back when you reopen the browser: after
  quitting, an update, or a crash. Tabs you haven't looked at yet load
  when you click them. Close every window yourself and it starts fresh.
  (This update is the last time your tabs won't come back.)
- Point at the blue line over the focused tab and it now says FOCUSED,
  in a wider pill.
