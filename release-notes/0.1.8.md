## What's new in 0.1.8

- Extensions: add them from a folder or from GitHub on the new
  Extensions page, turn their features on and off, and change their
  settings. Installed ones update themselves.
- Tab previews are simpler: rest the pointer on a tab to see its page
  just under it, with when you last looked at it. The previews button
  is gone.
- The app icon is flat, without the glass effect.
