## What's new in 0.1.9

- Tabs playing sound show a speaker. Click it to mute the tab; a muted
  tab keeps its crossed-out speaker until you unmute it.
- Address suggestions hang from the tab bar under the tab, like tab
  previews, and scroll if there are more than fit in the window.
- One click on the browser's tabs and buttons is enough, even when the
  window wasn't the active one.
- After ⌘T or +, you can type straight away: what you type goes into the
  new tab's address, even with a pop-out open.
- One click on an empty new tab edits its address.
- Pop-outs have rounded corners, keep showing their video when you switch
  tabs, and close with the tab they came from.
- The FOCUSED label on the active tab no longer widens when you point at
  it.
